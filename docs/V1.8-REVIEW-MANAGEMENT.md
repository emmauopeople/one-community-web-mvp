# Admin review management (v1.8)

Open Admin > Reviews. Pending is the default queue. Add decision notes, verify the experience and private contact details, tick verification, then Publish. Published reviews alone appear publicly and contribute to ratings.

Inactivate hides a review without deleting it. Soft delete moves it to Deleted and retains its body, deleting admin and timestamp. Restore to pending returns it for fresh verification; deleted reviews cannot be published directly. Reject remains available for unverifiable submissions. Every action requires notes and is version checked and audited atomically. The page includes a How to manage reviews guide.

Migration 006 preserves existing data and adds inactive/deleted states and deletion metadata. No permanent-delete endpoint exists. Restore clears current deletion metadata; the historical deletion remains in the audit log. Existing provider/email uniqueness remains in force after soft deletion.
